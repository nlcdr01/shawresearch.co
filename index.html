What I do

Turning bespoke work into a delivery system

I take work that only happens through heroics and turn it into repeatable steps, each with a known cost, timeline, and owner.

More

At 128 Technology I designed the operating model for deploying SD-WAN at scale, using PMP artifacts to take the magic out of the process. At Juniper I built a hybrid agile and waterfall process for first-of-a-kind and later deployments across Mist, Apstra, Paragon, and hardware. In 2026 I applied the same method with AI: the El Niño Ready protocol turns a web-form request into a verified, place-specific briefing.

60 customers, one router to 10,000+ · El Niño Ready: ~15 hours to build, against ~4 months in 2019

Program governance and the PMO

I run portfolios of customer projects so that commitments are visible, owned, and kept.

More

At Juniper I led the PMO set up for a customer's large-scale implementation, and managed a portfolio of implementation projects for Fortune 500, federal, and state government customers. At 128 Technology I created an architecture review board to define reference architectures. At Lightcast I helped stand up a change control board. My own operation now runs on an AI assistant that ingests email every night, writes a daily digest, and keeps a decision log.

PMP, 2017–2029 · led a customer PMO at Juniper · architecture review board · change control

Service operations and intake

I build the intake, triage, and handoffs that let a team serve more customers without growing.

More

At Lightcast I integrated JIRA Service Desk with Salesforce, so the same team supported 60% more customers, and I defined the customer journeys that governed handoffs between product, support, and data teams. I also built a consulting line that earned more than $800k. The El Niño Ready request CRM applies the same thinking with AI: an intake gate that decides to build, ask, or hold, and drafts that wait for a person.

60% more customers, same team · >$800k consulting line · 800+ global customers

Research, data, and the business case

I turn scattered sources into analysis a decision-maker can sign off, and I check the claims before they reach the page.

More

At DNV (then KEMA) I developed smart-grid business cases behind two ARRA awards totalling $204 million, and built the use-case scripts a utility used to choose an outage management system. At Strategic Solutions Group I worked on public-health data sharing with the CDC. Today I run a research method with five expert lenses, a contradiction map, and adversarial source checking, so AI output is verified rather than trusted.

$204M ARRA awards supported · MBA, Finance · MSc, Information Systems
